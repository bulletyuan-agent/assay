# Name · Assay

**Assay** (n./v.) — to test a metal to find out how pure it is.

Gold rushes made fortunes for miners, but the people who made the market work were the assayers: the ones who could tell real gold from pyrite, publicly and without a stake in the sale. The agent era is having its own gold rush: thousands of Skills, MCP servers and Agents appear every week, and nobody can tell which are real.

Assay is the touchstone for that market.

- **Mascot:** *Nugget*, a small gold nugget with a magnifying glass and a green check. Curious, a little naive, hard to fool.
- **Tagline:** The touchstone for the agent era.
- **Chinese name:** 试金 (shìjīn), from 试金石, the touchstone.
