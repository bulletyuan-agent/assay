Every gold rush needs an assayer.

每一场淘金热,都需要化验师。

今天的 Agent 栈像 1995 年的互联网:连接层已经跑通,供给在爆炸,却没人说得清什么值得信。接下来会发生什么,历史其实很一致。

Autonomy only scales as far as trust does.

自主性能走多远,取决于信任能走多远。

People will let an agent spend money, sign things and touch accounts only when someone independent has checked the work. Today, the people who grade agents are the people who sell them.

只有当独立的第三方检查过,人们才敢让 Agent 花钱、签约、动账户。而今天,给 Agent 打分的,恰恰是卖 Agent 的人。

Why a platform can't be the referee

为什么平台没法当裁判

Big platforms are great at distribution and security scanning, and we won't compete there. But rating is a different job, and it breaks when the rater has a side.

大平台擅长分发和安全扫描,这些我们不去争。但评级是另一回事:评分的人一旦有立场,评级就失效了。

A platform that also plays

既是平台、又是球员

  • Its own models, cloud and agents are hard to rank low
  • 自家的模型、云、Agent 很难被排低
  • Rating a competitor negatively is a business problem
  • 给竞争对手打差评是商务问题
  • "Best match" may mean "our match"
  • 「最佳推荐」可能只是「我们家的推荐」
  • Enterprises worry about context reuse
  • 企业担心上下文被复用

An independent assayer

独立的化验师

  • Paid by readers, not by the rated
  • 由读者付费,而不是被评者
  • Methods and raw data are public
  • 方法与原始数据公开
  • Publishes negative findings, with a notice period
  • 发布负面结论,但留通知期
  • Builds no agent of its own
  • 不做自己的 Agent

What Assay does

试金做什么

Three layers, built in this order. Each one is useful on its own and feeds the next.

三层,按这个顺序建。每一层单独有用,又喂给下一层。

1

Evaluate

评测

Reproducible tests: does it install, match its description, stay inside its permissions, avoid undeclared network calls, and finish real tasks?

可复现的测试:能不能装上、是否与描述一致、有没有越权、有没有未声明的外发请求、真实任务能不能完成?

2

Rate

评级

Open, versioned trust ratings with raw data attached. Every rating names the model, version and date it was earned on.

公开、带版本的信任评级,附原始数据。每个评级都注明是在哪个模型、版本、日期下得出的。

3

Catalogue

编目

A cross-ecosystem directory of what we tested, ordered by evidence instead of GitHub stars, which can be bought.

把测过的东西编成跨生态目录,按证据排序,而不是按可以刷的 GitHub star。

Phase 1 scope: office-scenario MCP servers and Skills (documents, sheets, calendar, mail), 20 to 30 items tested in depth. Closed-platform Agents come later, as task-based evaluations.

第一阶段范围:办公场景的 MCP server 与 Skill(文档、表格、日历、邮件),首批 20 到 30 个深测。闭源平台上的 Agent 之后再做,以「任务评测」的形式。

Five promises

五条承诺

Independence is not a slogan. It is a set of things you can check.

独立不是口号,而是一组你可以核对的事。

No money from the rated

不收被评者的钱

Revenue comes from readers and buyers of reports, never from the things we score.

收入来自读者和报告买家,绝不来自被我们打分的对象。

Open and rerunnable

公开、可复跑

Methods, scripts and raw data are public. If you can't reproduce it, don't trust it.

方法、脚本、原始数据全部公开。复现不了,就别信。

Bad news gets published

坏消息照发

Authors get a fair notice period, then the finding goes out. Evidence is kept.

先给作者合理的通知期,然后发布。证据留存。

Never a player

绝不当球员

We will not build a competing agent. A referee who plays is not a referee.

我们不做竞品 Agent。会踢球的裁判不是裁判。

Versioned and dated

带版本、带日期

Models change weekly. Every result says what it was tested on and when.

模型每周都在变。每个结果都写明测的是什么、什么时候测的。

Challengeable

可被挑战

Open an issue, dispute a method, nominate a target. Corrections are public too.

开 issue、质疑方法、推荐待测对象。更正同样公开。

路线图

不先建大平台。先发布真实的报告,看谁在意。
