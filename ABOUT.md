# About Assay

**Independent evaluation and trust ratings for the agent era.**

AI agents are becoming a layer of the internet. Skills, MCP servers and Agents are multiplying faster than anyone can read them, and the people shipping them are also the people grading them. History says what comes next: when supply explodes, the party that can be *trusted to sort it* captures the value. Yahoo organised the web, Google ranked it, Consumer Reports and Wirecutter told buyers what was worth buying.

Assay is that party for agents.

## What we do
1. **Evaluate.** Run reproducible tests on Skills and MCP servers: does it install, does it do what it says, does it stay inside its permissions, does it phone home.
2. **Rate.** Publish open, versioned trust ratings. Negative findings included.
3. **Catalogue.** The tested results become a cross-ecosystem directory, ordered by evidence instead of stars.

## What we promise
- No money from the things we rate.
- Methods and raw data are public, so anyone can rerun them.
- We publish bad news, after giving authors a fair notice period.
- We never ship an agent of our own. We are the referee, not a player.

## Why it matters
Autonomy only scales as far as trust does. People will let agents spend money, sign things and touch accounts only when someone independent has checked the work. Assay builds that checking layer in the open.
